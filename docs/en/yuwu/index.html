San Juan de Frías Reserve

Yuwú: the forest that endures.

218 hectares of tropical rainforest that we chose to protect, alongside the cattle farm.

The name

A tribute to the Yariguí people

The Yariguíes, a Carib people, lived in these forests of the Magdalena Medio in Santander. Their language disappeared in the early twentieth century. Yuwú is a reconstruction based on related languages, evoking dense woodland, the living forest. We take it as a tribute: this forest still stands where they lived.

The blue-billed curassow

Colombia's most threatened bird. And it still lives here.

Crax alberti is endemic to Colombia: it exists nowhere else on the planet. The IUCN classifies it as Critically Endangered, the last category before extinct in the wild. In 25 years it lost 92.8% of its habitat.

The project

Conserve and produce, as one system

Yuwú is not the opposite of Ganadería San Juan: it is its complement. One challenges us to discover how much value we can create by working with nature. The other reminds us how much value exists in what we decide not to touch.

Conservation

The 218 hectares stay protected, with a species inventory and camera trap monitoring.

Knowledge

Open doors for research, education and the communities around the forest.

Financing

We are exploring mechanisms to fund its long-term protection, without depending only on the goodwill of those who look after it.
