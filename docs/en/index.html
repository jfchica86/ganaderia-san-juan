How we operate

Our work starts with the soil and the grass

We want to be caretakers of the soil and managers of biomass. We observe how much forage grows, what the cattle eat, and how each paddock recovers. Grazing management connects the health of the pastures with animal production and the economics of the farm.

01 · Soil and grass

Grass is the raw material. The herder decides where the cattle go in, how long they stay, and when they leave.

02 · Modules

High-density rotations supported by water, fencing, observation and records, in units of about 250 hectares.

03 · Herders

We train herders with field judgment, drawing on local talent and with active participation of women.

04 · Technology

Individual identification, weight records and inventory monitoring, in service of decisions.

The garden

Three dimensions, not one

Our work on the farm is spiritual, relational and ecological. Almost all regenerative ranching brands stop at the third.

Spiritual

Wonder as an opportunity and gratitude as a daily practice. It is what gives the work meaning.

Relational

The farm as a meeting place: family, friends, institutions. A space to experiment and learn.

Ecological

Learning to think like an ecosystem. Soil, water, living fences, agroforestry.

Who we are

Honor our roots. Transform our future.

We believe the best way to honor what we have received is to dare to build something even better.

Ganadería San Juan comes from a family story, from a deep connection to the countryside, and from a question that stays with us: what would be possible if we learned to understand and manage our land in a fundamentally different way?

Our father spent many years of his life acquiring, working and caring for the farms we are fortunate to receive today. He left us much more than land and cattle. He left us a story of hard work, a family legacy and an extraordinary opportunity.

We want to honor that legacy.
